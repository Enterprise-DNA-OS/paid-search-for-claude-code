# A working desk without a front end

The five recurring jobs are reading evidence, deciding a budget, assigning a review, recording the decision and preparing a report. A database and the commands cover them. Read-only HTML gives the agency a meeting pack without a web application.

This base does not reproduce Optmyzr's live ad-platform connections, bid automation, account monitoring, proprietary recommendations, shopping optimisation or mobile interface. Its records are only as fresh as the last export. There is no offline phone capture, drag-and-drop planning, authenticated client portal or browser editing.

An operator can review exported evidence and carry out approved work separately in the advertising platform. Enterprise DNA can scope the live connections and team screens as part of their managed version. Agree and test those before retiring operational controls in the incumbent. The base is not a full drop-in replacement for every Optmyzr feature.
